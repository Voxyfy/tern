Tern 1.0.0

First release.

  • Teleport – click the map or search an address, press Go.
  • Route – stops on the map, road or walking directions from Apple Maps, avoid tolls or highways, round trips and loops.
  • Traffic pace – follow Apple's travel-time estimate instead of a fixed speed; "realistic pace" adds small variations.
  • Joystick – drag the knob or use the arrow keys.
  • GPX – open any GPX track and play it at your speed.
  • Navigation camera – the map follows the phone, heading-up and tilted during a trip.
  • Favorites and recent places, English and Turkish.
  • iOS 17 and later connect over a userspace tunnel, no password prompt. iOS 16 and earlier get the developer disk image mounted automatically.
